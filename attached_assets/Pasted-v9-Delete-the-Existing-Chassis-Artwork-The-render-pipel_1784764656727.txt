v9 — Delete the Existing Chassis Artwork
The render pipeline is proven correct.
Do not audit it again.
The current authored chassis shapes themselves are visually unacceptable.
They still use the same underlying design language:
central mass
+
multiple rounded protrusions
+
rough radial symmetry
+
soft continuous contour
This is why the game still looks like flowers and starfish.
The fix is now purely a shape-design rewrite.
Hard prohibition
Do not use any of the current v7 chassis polygon coordinates.
Delete them.
Do not “refine” them.
Do not preserve their silhouettes.
Start from zero.
 
⸻
 
1. Build chassis from primitive silhouette families that cannot become flowers
Use these mandatory base silhouettes:
INTERCEPTOR
long narrow fuselage

STRIKER
forward-pointing wedge

ARTILLERY
rear block + long barrel

TANK
wide rectangle

RAMMER
large front triangle

TURRET
base block + directional barrel

CARRIER
large elongated hull

SWARM
small dart

CONTROLLER
asymmetric body + antenna mast

ADAPTIVE
combination of two above
Do not begin with a circle.
Do not begin with an ellipse.
Do not begin with a radial polygon.
 
⸻
 
2. Enforce extreme aspect ratios
At least half of all chassis should have:
width / height >= 1.8
or:
height / width >= 1.8
Examples:
interceptor: 54 × 20
artillery:   64 × 18
tank:        42 × 34
rammer:      48 × 28
carrier:     58 × 32
A roughly 1:1 silhouette should be the exception.
The current enemies remain too close to circular.
 
⸻
 
3. Ban rounded radial appendage distribution
No chassis may have:
3 rounded lobes around a center
4 rounded lobes around a center
5 rounded lobes around a center
6 rounded lobes around a center
No equally spaced appendages.
No evenly distributed wings.
No “petal” geometry.
Directional units must be longitudinal.
 
⸻
 
4. Create front, middle, and rear zones
Every directional chassis must explicitly occupy:
FRONT ZONE
MIDDLE ZONE
REAR ZONE
Example:
ARTILLERY

FRONT            MIDDLE         REAR
───────────────▶[MOUNT][████████]
     barrel               chassis
Example:
INTERCEPTOR

      nose
       ▼
   ─────────
  / FUSELAGE \
 <            >
      ||||
     ENGINE
At least 60% of the visual mass should lie along one dominant axis.
 
⸻
 
5. No circular center motif
Remove:
central circle
central ring
core circle
inner orb
from the majority of chassis.
These details are one of the strongest reasons the game reads as “virus flowers.”
Use circles only where functionally necessary.
For example:
wheel
turret bearing
energy node
sensor dome
Not as the universal body center.
 
⸻
 
6. Use hard angular geometry
For the next version, temporarily ban curves.
Use only:
rectangles
trapezoids
triangles
straight-edged polygons
bars
No:
arc()
ellipse()
quadraticCurveTo()
bezierCurveTo()
This is temporary but important.
The goal is to force the visual language away from organic flowers.
Once the shapes read correctly, selective curves can be reintroduced later.
 
⸻
 
7. Use substantially different bounding boxes
The screenshot shows too many units occupying approximately the same circular footprint.
Required approximate bounds:
Interceptor   60×20
Striker       44×26
Artillery     70×18
Tank          40×36
Rammer        50×30
Turret        36×30
Carrier       62×38
Swarm         22×10
Controller    34×52
Adaptive      variable
Do not normalize these into a common radius.
The renderer should accept:
visualWidth
visualHeight
instead of deriving everything from R.
 
⸻
 
8. Make the unit function occupy the silhouette
The defining gameplay feature should dominate the entire shape.
Examples:
Artillery:
barrel = 40–50% of total width

Rammer:
prow = 35–50% of total mass

Carrier:
payload bays = visually obvious

Tank:
armor = outermost dominant geometry

Interceptor:
engines + fuselage dominate

Controller:
sensor mast visibly changes overall silhouette
Do not represent functionality using tiny interior lines or small decorative shapes.
At this scale, internal details disappear.
 
⸻
 
9. Remove organic overlays during final rendering
The screenshots still show soft curves and radial line effects.
Even if these are “only additive,” they reinforce the flower appearance.
Temporarily disable:
radial tendrils
curved interior lines
halo rings
central circles
symmetric decorative marks
organic veins
Keep only:
base chassis
one outline
simple glow
health bar

⸻
 
11. New acceptance criteria
Do not evaluate chassis one at a time.
Take a live screenshot containing at least six enemies.
Fail immediately if:
* more than one enemy resembles a flower
* more than one enemy resembles a starfish
* more than two enemies have a central circle
* more than two enemies have roughly 1:1 aspect ratio
* more than two enemies use radial symmetry
* the longest and widest enemies differ by less than 40%
* artillery does not visibly have a long barrel
* rammer does not visibly have a dominant front
* tank is not visibly boxier than interceptor
No subjective “✓ readable” statements.
The screenshot itself is the acceptance test.
 
⸻
 
The important correction now is:
v8 proved the pipeline works. It did not prove the artwork works.
Your latest screenshots show that the live game is indeed rendering the new system—but the new shapes were designed using essentially the same visual vocabulary as before. The next iteration needs to destroy that vocabulary entirely: no radial bodies, no central circles, no lobed silhouettes, extreme aspect-ratio diversity, and hard directional geometry.
